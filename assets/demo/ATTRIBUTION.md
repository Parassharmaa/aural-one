# Audio attribution

The video uses fresh human-voice clips from Freesound. Each current source page listed **CC0 1.0** when checked for this demo. Attribution is still provided here.

| Video scene | Recording | Creator | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Joyful voice | “Yippee - Multi-Take” | Sadiquecat | [Freesound #810904](https://freesound.org/s/810904/) |
| Calm voice | “Female Meditation Spoken Word” | Sample_Me | [Freesound #610046](https://freesound.org/s/610046/) |
| Surprised voice and still image | “Surfer dude - kit - Surprise n Shock kit 1” | Sadiquecat | [Freesound #818949](https://freesound.org/s/818949/) |

The source MP3 previews were trimmed and converted to 16 kHz mono WAV for model scoring and video. The video adjusts playback gain for consistent listening levels; no voice was synthesized. The included `surprise.wav` is the **exact byte-identical file** scored by the public model for the still image (SHA-256 `a543aa3895072acb68a81ff34f3278fa9edc178ea1b8d63dbf8ef8ebf31b0103`). Source files and hashes for the editable video are recorded in its `audio_sources.json`.

The three video clips were selected after seeing model outputs. This is an illustrative demo, not an unbiased evaluation. The complete earlier fresh-clip results, including misidentified clips, remain in the local source project.
