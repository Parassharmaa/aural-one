# Audio credits

Human voice: **Kara Shallenberg**, reading *Alice's Adventures in Wonderland* by **Lewis Carroll**, LibriVox version 2 (2010).

- [LibriVox catalog and reader credits](https://librivox.org/alices-adventures-in-wonderland-by-lewis-carroll-4/)
- [Internet Archive source collection](https://archive.org/details/alices_adventures_1003)
- [Chapter 1 recording](https://archive.org/download/alices_adventures_1003/alices_adventures_01_carroll_64kb.mp3): excerpt 23.04–34.56 seconds.
- [Chapter 7 recording](https://archive.org/download/alices_adventures_1003/alices_adventures_07_carroll_64kb.mp3): excerpt 14.00–29.96 seconds.

LibriVox identifies the recordings as public domain in the USA. The work is the original English novel published in 1865. No voice was synthesized in the final film. Source excerpts were converted to 16 kHz mono WAV for the model. Playback copies were normalized to −18 LUFS, with a −1.8 dBTP ceiling. The model received the original excerpt WAVs, whose hashes are verified in the response file. Source speech plays without the music bed.

Music and transition effects: original deterministic synthesis created for this film by `scripts/make_score.py`; no external music samples.

Fonts: DM Sans, Instrument Serif, IBM Plex Mono from the Google Fonts repository, under the bundled SIL Open Font License notices in `assets/fonts/`.
